Behind the scenes

Our Founders

A great brand offers depth, experience, resonance and soul

The Mott 32 story is led by a team with deep experience across hospitality, design and restaurant operations. Their shared approach brings together Chinese culinary heritage, international service and immersive interiors.

Co-founder and Managing Director

Xuan Mu

Xuan has helped guide the restaurant group from the first Hong Kong address into a global collection. Her role connects brand strategy, guest experience and the details that make each Mott 32 feel personal to its city.

Co-founder

Malcolm Wood

Malcolm's hospitality background shapes the brand's point of view: destination restaurants with strong design, exacting service and a commitment to more responsible ways of operating.

Co-founder

Matt Reid

Matt brings commercial and operational discipline to the group, supporting the systems that let the Mott 32 experience travel while keeping a clear sense of craft and locality.

The brand

Built from hospitality, design and cuisine

Mott 32 connects refined Chinese cooking with interiors that reference a city's history, materials and rhythm. The founders' work is visible in the way each restaurant balances theatre with warmth.
