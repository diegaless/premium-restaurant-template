Authentic Asian Cuisine

We embrace flavours and ingredients from around Asia and the world

Around the globe, Mott 32 has won over 100 awards for food, drinks and design. The kitchen combines modern technique with Cantonese, Beijing and Szechuan influences, using premium ingredients and time-honoured recipes.
