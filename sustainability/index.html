What defines us

Sustainability

Responsible sourcing, thoughtful menus and lower-impact operations

Mott 32's approach to hospitality includes a practical commitment to the environment, suppliers and the communities around each restaurant. The goal is to keep the experience refined while making better choices behind the scenes.

Mindful dining

Circular thinking in a luxury restaurant setting

The group prioritises local sourcing where possible, sustainable seafood choices, plant-led menu options and a clear stance against endangered species and cage-farmed animal products.
