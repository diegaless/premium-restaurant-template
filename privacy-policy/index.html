Privacy Policy

Privacy and personal data

Mott 32 collects personal information when guests make enquiries, request reservations, subscribe to marketing updates or contact the restaurant group directly. This can include contact details, reservation preferences and correspondence needed to respond to the request.

How information is used

Information is used to manage reservations, answer enquiries, improve guest service, send opted-in updates and support restaurant operations. Data is kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed for those purposes or to meet legal obligations.

Service providers

Reservation platforms, email services, analytics providers and professional advisers may process limited information on behalf of the brand. These partners are expected to protect information and use it only for the services requested.

Cookies

The website uses necessary cookies for basic functionality and may use analytics or marketing cookies where consent has been given. Cookie preferences can be updated at any time using the cookie settings link in the footer.

Your choices

Guests can request access, correction or deletion of personal information where applicable, and can opt out of marketing communications by using unsubscribe links or contacting the restaurant group.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact info@mott32.com.
